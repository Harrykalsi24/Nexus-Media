About Nexus Media

We’re Nexus Media, a web engineering & performance marketing agency combining custom, sub-second websites, automated review capture systems, and measurable local ad funnels for brick-and-mortar companies across Canada. Truth > Aesthetics. Clean code over bloated templates. In-house logic over fragile plugins. If you want measurable local growth, we’re in.

We engineer custom websites, review capture systems, local SEO, and paid funnels.

We engineer interfaces.

Interfaces: Hand-coded, semantic, accessible (WCAG AA/AAA) web development with sub-second load times and zero builder bloat.

Review Systems: End-to-end customer review capture workflows, QR counter stands, and automated negative feedback interception before public posting.

Local SEO: Google Business Profile audit, map pack ranking optimization, Canadian citation alignment, and structured JSON-LD schema.

Paid Funnels: High-intent Google Search, Local Services Ads (LSA), and geo-fenced Meta campaigns engineered for verified phone calls and store visits.
